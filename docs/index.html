How to read the payout and cost

The ordinary payout floor is the minimum payout under normal settlement if every order fills. The conservative floor also allows for exceptional settlement. The simulated net lower bound credits the conservative payout on matched quantities and subtracts the cost of all simulated fills.

Synthetic holdout

Brier score

Measures how far the predicted probabilities were from the actual outcomes. Lower is better.

A well-calibrated 70% forecast comes true about 70% of the time. The diagonal represents perfect calibration. The numbers show how many forecasts are in each group.
